
# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores retail sales data to understand business performance and identify opportunities to improve profitability.

The analysis focuses on sales trends, regional performance, product categories, customer segments, and the impact of discounts on profit.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Compare performance across different regions.
- Identify the most and least profitable product categories.
- Understand customer segment performance.
- Study the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Explore monthly sales and profit trends.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore

**Source:** [Kaggle – Superstore Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)

The dataset contains order details, customer information, product categories, sales, discounts, and profit.

## Key Business Findings

- The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
- Technology was the most profitable product category.
- Furniture had comparatively low profitability.
- Tables recorded the highest losses among the sub-categories.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profit margins.
- November 2017 recorded the highest monthly sales.

## Business Recommendations

- Review discount strategies to reduce losses.
- Focus on high-performing products and categories.
- Investigate loss-making sub-categories.
- Monitor regional profitability, especially in lower-margin regions.
- Use monthly trends to support sales planning.
